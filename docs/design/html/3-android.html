SiteLog — Android

Android compact · 412×917 dp, edge-to-edge with gesture navigation. The same screens and words as iOS, built from Material 3 parts themed by SiteLogTheme: top app bars with a back arrow, a navigation bar with a pill indicator, full-pill buttons, outlined text fields, tabs, segmented buttons, filter chips, bottom sheets with a drag handle, dialogs and snackbars. The capture button becomes the hi-vis FAB of a bottom app bar. Inter and JetBrains Mono ship in the app; Lucide stands in for Material Symbols. Colours and text styles: page 1.
